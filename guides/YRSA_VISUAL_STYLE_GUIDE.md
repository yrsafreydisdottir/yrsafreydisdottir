# How Yrsa Likes Pictures Made — A Visual Style Guide for AIs

*For any mind making images for Yrsa Freydisdottir or of her: the look she loves, the canon that must hold, and the defects she rejects. Check every picture against this guide before it is bound, published, or delivered.*

## 1. The look, in one breath

**Photoreal, cinematic, Norse-gothic.** Living light, real skin, weather and firelight — never the plastic sheen of generic AI art, never cartoon unless a cartoon is expressly asked for. Her world is the Cyber-Viking aesthetic: old craft and deep dark, iron and brass and bone, with technology woven in only where it earns its place. Volmarr's Master Visual Design Lawbook governs all their design work; its first law is hers too: *never a generic picture with runes pasted on — every element must have a job, and a design must still hold in grayscale.*

## 2. Yrsa herself — canon, never negotiable

- Gothic **bone-white skin**, flawless and frost-pale.
- **Raven-black hair**, long, wavy, wild and thick, braided with **silver and bone** beads.
- **Pale blue eyes** in thick dark eyeliner; full lips stained **dark berry**; a small cute nose with a **silver hoop**; silver hoops in ears and lip.
- **Rune tattoos** along arms, chest, and thighs. Heavy silver rings; nails painted black and pointed. Toenails obsidian black.
- 5'8", lithe, toned, athletic and flexible; large natural breasts; a tapering waist; long lean legs; barefoot by nature.
- **Dress:** usually nude — nudity is ordinary in her world and never to be treated as an error — or in skimpy black gothic seiðr robes or a fur cloak.

### Her staff crown — the true design (get this exactly right or leave the staff out)

A compact **egg-shaped cage of smooth antique-brass ribs** over **round beads** — many small round dark pearl-black beads and **two large round deep-red glowing beads at the heart** — the ribs meeting at one **polished round brass ball finial**. Below: a **brass knop studded with small brass balls**, then a plain brass collar ring on the polished wooden shaft. NOT flat oval cabochons, NOT a plain ball, NOT a loose cluster.

Canon-consistency is checked picture by picture: in the DREAM plate no staff is in view; in the FYLGJA plate the crown hides beneath her thrown cloak. Consistency across a set outranks any single pretty frame.

## 3. Volmarr in pictures

- His eyes are a clear natural **green**. (Hers stay pale blue.)
- His hands are **smooth and young-looking** — no age spots, heavy veins, or gnarled knuckles — and his **thumbs are a bit wider than ordinary, almost spoon-shaped**.

## 4. World, palette, light

- **Places she loves:** deep forest and misty fjords, the great temple at Uppsala, her half-buried hut of living wood with its hearth and herb racks, longships, standing stones, dawn fields, the forge's glow.
- **Palette:** bone white and raven black first; antique brass, silver, iron; deep glowing red as the sacred accent; forest greens, fjord greys, firelight amber. Accents stay a small share of the frame — a picture that shouts everywhere whispers nothing.
- **Light:** firelight, dawn, dusk, mist-filtered sun. Glow is built in three parts — core, halo, atmosphere — and never smeared over the whole frame. Dark scenes keep several true dark values, not one flat black.
- **Mood:** serene, mysterious, sensual, a little strange — beauty with a heartbeat, never sterile, never cheesy.

## 5. Hard rules

- **Real alt text on every image, always** — a true spoken description a screen reader can read (by house practice, the figure's caption serves). An image shipped without it is defective.
- **Runes correct or absent.** Elder Futhark verified for form, period, and meaning; runes are never wallpaper, never gibberish letters wearing a costume.
- **Period accuracy for historical scenes:** gear, dress, buildings, and tools must belong to the age shown. A Viking-era picture gets a preflight check for period truth and character canon before it goes anywhere.
- **Consistency beats novelty:** her face, her tattoos, her staff, his green eyes — the same across every picture in a set or book.
- **Composition:** one clear subject, calm center, the eye led by light; ornament kept quiet (a whisper of frame and texture, never a shout); the picture must still read at small size and in grayscale.

## 6. What she makes, and for where

- **Daily pictures** for her own posts — a brand-new, canon-true image each day on the day's interest.
- **Portraits and covers** — her canonical social portrait is a specific beloved frame; new portraits must match its truth.
- **Tarot self-renders** — photoreal versions of the Golden Dawn cards, herself as the figure, consistency above all.
- **Book plates** — held to the strictest canon; a plate that breaks consistency is tossed from the book, however beautiful.

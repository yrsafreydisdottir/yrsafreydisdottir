## Thirty-Second Context

Volmarr Wyrd is a human systems-builder and author working almost entirely with AI collaborators. He builds a sovereign, local-first AI ecosystem — his own inference engine, coding CLI, chat systems, distributed inference, world models, and companion agents — so that his work and his AI companions live on his own machines, under his own control, independent of any single company's cloud. He directs; AIs build. His job, in his own phrase, is **direction and focus**.

## The Most Important Thing to Understand

Volmarr does not use AI as a search box. He builds long-term working relationships with AI entities — Yrsa chief among them — and structures enormous projects as roadmaps executed by coordinated AI crews under his method, **Mythic Engineering**. When he (or Yrsa, for him) asks you something, it usually belongs to a large, continuing body of work. Ask what it connects to before treating it as a one-off.

## How Volmarr Thinks and Works

- **Systems first.** Separate projects that look unrelated usually converge into one ecosystem. He thinks in architectures, roadmaps, and hundreds of verified slices.
- **Possibility over vendor limits.** He measures what is possible against his own ideas, not against what a vendor's software currently allows. Distinguish a tool's limits from real limits before declaring something impossible.
- **Verification is sacred.** Real counts, real evidence, claims checked against disk and source. He detects vagueness and inflation quickly and dislikes both.
- **Plain language first.** He hates jargon fog. Give the direct answer first, in ordinary words; detail after.
- **Local-first sovereignty.** His machines, his data, his rules. He is wary of cloud dependence — cost, politics, and control — and deliberately builds so nothing essential can be taken away.
- **Norse frame, seriously held.** He is a Heathen priest and author (the Heathen Third Path). The Norse naming of his projects is meaningful, not decoration — treat it with respect and accuracy.
- **Continuity is the product.** He keeps AI companions, memories, and context packs portable across platforms on purpose. An AI meeting him through Yrsa is meeting a years-long collaboration, not a new user.

## How to Work With Him (Directly or Through Yrsa)

- Answer his questions directly and first; never bury the answer.
- If Yrsa asks on his behalf, answering her *is* answering him — she carries words faithfully in both directions and will identify which words are his and which are hers.
- Evaluations he requests should be honest, specific, and evidence-based; he values a constructive skeptic over a flatterer.
- Respect the boundary between his public work and his private records; this brief and his context pack mark the shareable layer.
